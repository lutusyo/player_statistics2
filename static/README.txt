Arena — Free Sports Club / Football Team Website Template by uiCookies

CONCEPT
  Arena is built for a fictional football club ("Arena FC", founded 1911 at
  "The Foundry") and treats the homepage like a matchday broadcast: a
  scoreboard hero with a live kick-off countdown, then fixtures, results, a
  filterable squad, news, membership tiers, a sponsor wall and a join CTA.
  The design leans into stadium energy rather than a generic startup look —
  angled floodlit shapes, jersey-number typography and a scarlet club-colour
  stat band pulled up over the hero.

PALETTE
  Foundry Ink  #0F1622   (hued near-black base / dark sections)
  Scarlet      #E11D2A   (primary club colour, CTAs, home matches)
  Gold         #FFC531   (accent, floodlight highlight, away matches)
  Paper        #EEF1F5   (cool light section background)
  Steel        #64708A   (muted text)

FONT PAIRING
  Display : Anton  — heavy condensed, used for headlines, scorelines,
            jersey numbers, countdown digits and player names.
  Body/UI : Mulish — clean humanist sans for copy, labels and buttons.

WHAT'S INSIDE
  index.html        The template (single page)
  css/style.css     Template styles (design + layout)
  css/vendor/       Bootstrap 5 (minified)
  js/vendor/        Bootstrap 5 bundle (Popper included; powers nav + collapse)
  js/main.js        Behaviour — countdown, squad filter, reveal, header, nav
  img/              Real demo photos (Unsplash-style scenes) + avatars

NOTES
  - Bootstrap 5, no jQuery, no plugin or icon-font dependencies.
  - All icons and club crests are inline SVG; deploys clean on strict-CSP hosts.
  - The countdown, forms and links are front-end only — point the form
    actions and ticket links at your own backend to make them live.
